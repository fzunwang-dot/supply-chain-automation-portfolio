# Fazun Wang — Supply Chain Analytics & Automation Portfolio

This repository contains the public, anonymized portfolio website for Fazun Wang. It presents business-first analytics and automation work across supply chain operations, merchandising, product data, pricing, and recurring reporting workflows.

## Featured case studies

1. Member Invoice Splitting & Validation System
2. Merchandising Purchase Tracker & KPI Dashboard
3. Market-Based Bedding Pricing Strategy
4. Product Catalogue Reconciliation & SKU Status Audit
5. Promotion, P&P & Price-Tag Automation

## Core capabilities

- Excel/VBA workflow automation and controlled output generation
- Data reconciliation, normalized identifiers, and duplicate-safe matching
- Exception reporting, audit fields, and validation controls
- Supply chain documents, shipment allocation, and member billing
- Product catalogue governance, competitor benchmarking, and pricing analysis
- KPI reporting and bilingual English/French deliverables

## Confidentiality

All examples are reconstructed and anonymized. This repository excludes internal workbooks, raw data, member and vendor records, proprietary pricing, company paths, and production VBA modules.
