Flagship case study · Supply chain

Member Invoice Splitting & Validation System

Built a controlled workflow to clean shipment documents, reconcile commercial and logistics records, merge customs charges, enrich member information, and generate standardized member-level invoice and bill-of-lading outputs.

3Source datasets
2 per memberInvoice + BOL tabs
~94%Process-time reduction

Business problem

  • Repeated manual copying across shipment documents
  • Duplicate and ambiguous product lines
  • Inconsistent member allocation and formatting
  • High risk of missing customs or logistics fields

Controls designed

  • PDF-paste cleanup and fuzzy header detection
  • Composite keys using item, SKU, vendor, PO, quantity, and price
  • Strict duplicate-aware customs matching
  • Audit columns for match status and match key

Product Catalogue Reconciliation & SKU Status Audit

Created an exception-based comparison tool that reconciles internal catalogue records against a vendor list using normalized model keys, separates status decisions from price checks, and routes results into action-specific review queues.

3,600Internal catalogue rows
1,937Vendor records
1,260Actionable review items

Automation logic

  • Auto-detects headers within the first five rows
  • Normalizes model numbers into clean comparison keys
  • Flags duplicate vendor keys instead of forcing matches
  • Separates product-status actions from price exceptions

Output queues

  • Enable or disable internal products
  • Remove products no longer found at vendor
  • Confirm uncertain status with vendor
  • Review vendor-only records for product creation
